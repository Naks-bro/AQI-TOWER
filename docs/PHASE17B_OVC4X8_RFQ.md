## Part B — FORMASORB Inquiry

### B.1 Classification: BUY LATER / QUOTE NOW

FORMASORB is needed for Phase 17B+ HCHO breakthrough testing only. It must NOT be ordered until:
1. Institutional safety approval for HCHO gas handling is obtained.
2. Phase 17A clean-air pressure-drop testing is complete.

However, quoting now is advised so that lead time and India availability are known before that phase begins.

### B.2 Verified product identification

- **Product:** Calgon Carbon FORMASORB
- **Grade:** The Calgon Carbon datasheet identifies this as an impregnated coconut-shell activated carbon for formaldehyde and aldehyde removal. The datasheet lists 4×8 and 6×12 US mesh options.
- **For this project:** 4×8 US mesh grade is preferred (same mesh as OVC 4×8, allowing direct pressure-drop comparison at the same geometry).
- **Caution:** Do not assume that any "FORMASORB" listing refers to a formaldehyde-specific product without confirming against the Calgon Carbon datasheet (the tradename appears on multiple specialized media). Confirm the exact product identity before ordering.
- **Source:** https://www.calgoncarbon.com/products/formasorb/ (Kuraray / Calgon Carbon official)

### B.3 FORMASORB supplier routes

Same manufacturer routes as OVC 4×8 (Part A, Routes 1–3). FORMASORB is not expected to be available through Jacobi India (it is a proprietary impregnated grade specific to Kuraray/Calgon Carbon).
