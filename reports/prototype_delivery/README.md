## Short presentation script

We are developing a tower that draws air through dust filters and returns filtered air. We already have computer airflow studies, packaging models and measurement tools. We have not built or physically tested the tower yet. We are asking for support to assemble one safe indoor prototype using standard components and a local workshop, then measure what it actually does. The outdoor chain of cleaner-air zones is our future research goal, not a proven result. We can show the digital model today and, if approved, arrange a separately labelled reference-appliance demonstration while developing our own core.
